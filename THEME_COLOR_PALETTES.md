## Color Science

### Midnight Theme - Why It's Special

**Color Psychology:**
- Deep blue-black: Calming, reduces eye fatigue
- Cyan accent: Modern, energetic without harshness
- Professional blue: Trustworthy, clear
- Off-white text: Perfect contrast, easy to read

**Gaming Benefits:**
- Reduces blooming on dark scenes
- Cyan accent matches modern game UIs
- Less blue light (compared to bright blue)
- Minimal color shift in low light

**Visual Comfort:**
- Meets AAA WCAG contrast standards
- Designed for 2-4 hour+ sessions
- Reduces pupil dilation in dark environments
- Minimal strain on rod/cone cells
