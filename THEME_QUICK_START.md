# 🎨 Quick Start: Theme Selection Guide
Project name: OpenFlux (internal targets/bundle still named "Flux").

## How to Choose Your Theme

### Step 1: Launch OpenFlux
The app starts with the **Midnight** theme (gaming-optimized default).

### Step 2: Go to Settings
Click the **⚙️ Settings** tab at the bottom of the sidebar.

### Step 3: Find Theme Section
Scroll to **"🎨 Appearance & Themes"** section.

### Step 4: Preview Themes
You'll see 7 beautiful theme cards:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🌙 Midnight     ✓ Selected               │
│ Color preview: [Blue] [Cyan] [White]      │
│ "Gaming-optimized, minimal eye strain"     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ⚡ Neon                                     │
│ Color preview: [Magenta] [Cyan] [White]   │
│ "Vibrant, high-contrast colors"            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [More themes below...]                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 5: Click to Switch
Click any theme card to see the entire app change instantly!

### Step 6: Preference Saves
Your choice is saved automatically. Next time you launch, your theme is there.

## Tips & Tricks

### 💡 Multi-Monitor Setup
- Use **Midnight** for main gaming/coding display
- Use **Minimal** for bright side monitor

### 🎯 Time-Based Switching
- Morning/Day: **Minimal** (bright and professional)
- Evening: **Sunset** (warm and cozy)
- Night: **Midnight** (minimal blue light)

### 👥 Sharing Your Preference
Let others know which theme you use:
- "I use OpenFlux's Midnight theme"
- "Midnight is perfect for my workflow"
- "Gaming with Midnight theme"

### 🎮 Gaming Setup
1. Set **Midnight** as your default
2. Use cyan accents throughout your setup
3. Match gaming peripherals to the theme colors
4. Coordinated aesthetic = better immersion

### 📸 Screenshots
- Use **Minimal** for professional screenshots
- Use **Midnight** for gaming/technical content
- Use **Neon** for attention-grabbing demos

## Troubleshooting

### Theme Won't Save?
- Restart OpenFlux and it will appear
- Themes save to UserDefaults automatically

### Colors Look Wrong?
- Check your display color profile
- Restart the app to reset colors
- All themes should look crisp and clear

### Animation Too Slow?
- 0.3s transition is standard
- Can't be adjusted (built-in animation)
- Ensures smooth color transitions

### Theme Doesn't Fit My Setup?
- Try different themes
- Choose based on your lighting environment
- Midnight works in almost all situations
